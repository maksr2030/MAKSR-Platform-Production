Licensing
Preview architecture

License technology with rights, scope and evidence made explicit.

Structure technology and intellectual property licensing around defined rights, fields of use, territories, evidence and ongoing obligations.

Create Licensing MandateExplore Opportunities
Scope

A license is a bounded right, not a vague permission.

The transaction must distinguish what is licensed, where it may be used, for what purpose, by whom and under what continuing conditions.

01

Exclusive scope

Exclusivity can be tied to territory, field, channel or other defined boundaries.

02

Non-exclusive scope

Rights can be granted without preventing additional licensing where the agreement permits.

03

Commercial deployment

Use rights, support, updates and operating obligations are separated from ownership transfer.

04

White-label or embedded use

Branding and embedding rights require explicit boundaries and evidence of authorized use.

Transaction route

A staged route with explicit access and decision points.

The interface is organized around transaction state, evidence and authority. Sensitive information is disclosed only when the current stage permits it.

Discover

Define the relevant opportunity or mandate without unnecessary disclosure.

Qualify

Confirm institutional fit, identity and authority before deeper access.

Access

Apply confidentiality and permission controls before sensitive information is released.

Evaluate

Review evidence, value, Added Value, risk and transaction readiness.

Structure

Translate the objective into terms, conditions, milestones and approvals.

Close or Activate

Complete transfer, investment, licensing or partnership activation with evidence.

Control principles

Licensing controls the right to use without silently transferring ownership.

  • The licensed asset and version are identifiable.
  • Territory, field of use, term and sublicensing rules are explicit.
  • Support, update and dependency obligations are separated from license grant.
  • Evidence of rights remains available for audit and renewal decisions.
Decision frame

Every route must remain explainable.

Valuation, Added Value, risk, readiness and evidence are separated so one strong dimension cannot hide a material weakness in another.
