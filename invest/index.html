Scope

Investment routes must reflect stage and control.

The same capital amount can imply very different rights, risks and execution obligations. MAKSR keeps the investment structure and operating evidence visible as separate decision factors.

01

Pre-revenue

Technology value is assessed without inventing commercial traction that does not exist.

02

Early stage

Execution capability, ownership and evidence quality matter alongside market potential.

03

Growth

Financial quality, concentration, operating scale and transaction structure become increasingly material.

04

Strategic capital

Buyer-specific synergies and Added Value are examined separately from standalone valuation.

Transaction route

A staged route with explicit access and decision points.

The interface is organized around transaction state, evidence and authority. Sensitive information is disclosed only when the current stage permits it.

Discover

Define the relevant opportunity or mandate without unnecessary disclosure.

Qualify

Confirm institutional fit, identity and authority before deeper access.

Access

Apply confidentiality and permission controls before sensitive information is released.

Evaluate

Review evidence, value, Added Value, risk and transaction readiness.

Structure

Translate the objective into terms, conditions, milestones and approvals.

Close or Activate

Complete transfer, investment, licensing or partnership activation with evidence.

Control principles

No investment claim without its basis.

  • Commercial metrics remain source-linked and period-specific.
  • Pre-revenue opportunities are not forced into revenue-based narratives.
  • Investor rights and authority are part of the transaction structure, not a footnote.
  • Added Value can support a strategic case but cannot override weak evidence.
Decision frame

Every route must remain explainable.

Valuation, Added Value, risk, readiness and evidence are separated so one strong dimension cannot hide a material weakness in another.

StageIs the evidence appropriate for the maturity of the venture?
EconomicsWhat is verified, forecast and assumption-based?

Private intake

Describe the investment thesis, not just the cheque size.

A private mandate can define sector, stage, geography, ticket size, readiness and strategic objective before matching begins.
