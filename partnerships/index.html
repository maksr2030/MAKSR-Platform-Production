Strategic Partnerships
Preview architecture

Form strategic partnerships without forcing an acquisition.

Structure commercial, technology, distribution, execution, research and market-access partnerships around explicit objectives, contribution and authority.

Create Partnership MandateExplore Opportunities
Scope

Partnership is a transaction path of its own.

A strategic relationship should not be squeezed into investment or acquisition language when the real objective is access, capability, distribution, execution or joint development.

01

Commercial

Market access, customer development or commercialization with defined responsibilities.

02

Technology

Integration, co-development or technology access governed by intellectual property boundaries.

03

Distribution & Regional

Territory, channel and execution responsibilities made explicit before launch.

04

Research & Data

Collaboration involving research, data or evidence with controlled rights and use.

Transaction route

A staged route with explicit access and decision points.

The interface is organized around transaction state, evidence and authority. Sensitive information is disclosed only when the current stage permits it.

Discover

Define the relevant opportunity or mandate without unnecessary disclosure.

Qualify

Confirm institutional fit, identity and authority before deeper access.

Access

Apply confidentiality and permission controls before sensitive information is released.

Evaluate

Review evidence, value, Added Value, risk and transaction readiness.

Structure

Translate the objective into terms, conditions, milestones and approvals.

Close or Activate

Complete transfer, investment, licensing or partnership activation with evidence.

Control principles

A partnership should state what each side can do and decide.

  • Objectives, scope and excluded activities are explicit.
  • Data and intellectual property use follow permission and purpose.
  • Commercial rights are separated by territory, channel or field when needed.
  • Termination and transition obligations are treated as operating requirements.
Decision frame

Every route must remain explainable.

Valuation, Added Value, risk, readiness and evidence are separated so one strong dimension cannot hide a material weakness in another.

ObjectiveWhat business outcome is the partnership solving?
ContributionWhat does each side provide?
RightsWhat can each side use, sell, access or represent?
AuthorityWho can approve changes and exceptions?
Private intake

Start with the partnership objective.

A private mandate can define the partner profile, geography, capability, commercial objective and required evidence before introductions begin.
