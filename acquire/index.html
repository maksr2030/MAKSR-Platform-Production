Scope

What can enter an acquisition route?

MAKSR is designed for technology-led transactions where ownership, technical state, operating dependency and transferability matter as much as price.

01

Technology platforms

Operating or pre-scale platforms where code, infrastructure and ownership require structured review.

02

Intellectual property

Software rights, algorithms, registered rights and other transferable technology assets.

03

Digital businesses

Technology-led businesses where transaction value depends on operating continuity and customer context.

04

Strategic technology assets

Assets whose buyer-specific value may exceed their standalone market value.

Transaction route

A staged route with explicit access and decision points.

The interface is organized around transaction state, evidence and authority. Sensitive information is disclosed only when the current stage permits it.

Discover

Define the relevant opportunity or mandate without unnecessary disclosure.

Qualify

Confirm institutional fit, identity and authority before deeper access.

Access

Apply confidentiality and permission controls before sensitive information is released.

Evaluate

Review evidence, value, Added Value, risk and transaction readiness.

Structure

Translate the objective into terms, conditions, milestones and approvals.

Close or Activate

Complete transfer, investment, licensing or partnership activation with evidence.

Control principles

Acquisition access is earned, not assumed.

  • Public discovery never requires publication of sensitive ownership or financial evidence.
  • Qualified access can be staged by identity, mandate fit and confidentiality state.
  • Valuation is separated from buyer-specific Added Value.
  • Closing requires evidence of transfer, acceptance and unresolved obligations.
Decision frame

Every route must remain explainable.

Valuation, Added Value, risk, readiness and evidence are separated so one strong dimension cannot hide a material weakness in another.
