Overview

Publicly safe information first.

The public opportunity page is intentionally designed to separate discovery information from confidential evidence. Sensitive details are disclosed only after identity, fit, confidentiality and seller approval conditions are satisfied.

Business

Commercial information follows permission.

Business model, customers, market position and traction are not displayed in the public design preview.

Technology

Architecture without source disclosure.

The production interface will present only the technical facts authorized for the current access level, while repository evidence and sensitive architecture remain controlled.

Financials

Financial evidence is permissioned.

No revenue, valuation or forecast data is shown publicly unless explicitly approved for publication.

Ownership & Intellectual Property

Claims must open into evidence.

The final interface links ownership confidence to chain-of-title evidence, assignments, registrations and third-party dependencies rather than presenting a generic verified badge.

Evaluation

Explain the score or do not show it.

Evaluation is designed around inspectable dimensions. Live scores will include evidence, assumptions, reviewer authority, last update and history.
