How MAKSR works

Discovery, qualification, controlled access, evaluation, negotiation and closing remain separate so privacy, evidence and authority are preserved.

Operating model

Discovery does not equal disclosure.

The public layer explains enough to qualify interest. Sensitive evidence moves only when access conditions are met.

01

Discover

Search or match against criteria using information approved for the current audience.

02

Qualify

Confirm identity, institutional fit and authority before deeper access.

03

Evaluate

Separate valuation, Added Value, ownership, evidence, readiness and risk.

Next step

Continue through the appropriate transaction route.
