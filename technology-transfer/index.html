Scope

Transferability is more than ownership.

A buyer or receiving institution needs sufficient rights, knowledge, dependencies and operating context to make the transferred technology usable after handover.

01

Rights transfer

Assignments and permitted use are aligned with the actual transferred assets.

02

Knowledge transfer

Architecture, operating procedures and critical context move with the technology.

03

Technical handover

Repositories, environments, dependencies and credentials follow a controlled checklist.

04

Acceptance & continuity

The receiving party verifies operation and unresolved obligations before closure.

Transaction route

A staged route with explicit access and decision points.

The interface is organized around transaction state, evidence and authority. Sensitive information is disclosed only when the current stage permits it.

Discover

Define the relevant opportunity or mandate without unnecessary disclosure.

Qualify

Confirm institutional fit, identity and authority before deeper access.

Access

Apply confidentiality and permission controls before sensitive information is released.

Evaluate

Review evidence, value, Added Value, risk and transaction readiness.

Structure

Translate the objective into terms, conditions, milestones and approvals.

Close or Activate

Complete transfer, investment, licensing or partnership activation with evidence.

Control principles

A transfer is complete only when the receiving side can verify what it received.

  • Transferred assets are inventoried and versioned.
  • Knowledge and dependency gaps are recorded rather than hidden.
  • Acceptance criteria are agreed before final closure.
  • Unresolved obligations survive as explicit transition items.
Decision frame

Every route must remain explainable.

Valuation, Added Value, risk, readiness and evidence are separated so one strong dimension cannot hide a material weakness in another.

RightsCan the receiving party lawfully use the transferred technology?
KnowledgeCan the system be understood and operated after handover?
DependenciesWhat third parties or infrastructure remain necessary?
AcceptanceWhat evidence proves the transfer works?
Private intake

Plan the transition before moving the assets.

A private transfer mandate can define the technology, receiving environment, knowledge obligations and acceptance standard before detailed disclosure.
